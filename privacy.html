Privacy Policy

Effective November 16, 2026 · Last updated November 17, 2026

Shadow is a self-improvement app where you race Shadow, a small version of you that shows up every day. This policy explains what information the app uses, why, where it's kept, and how you can see or delete it. We've kept it short and plain.

The short version

  • We only collect what Shadow needs to work: your answers, your progress, and your account.
  • No ads. No tracking across other apps or websites. We never sell your information.
  • You sign in with Apple, so your progress is backed up and comes back on a new phone.
  • You can delete your account and everything saved with it at any time, from inside the app.

1. Who we are

Shadow is made by Efeoghene Peter Oroh, an independent developer in Canada ("we", "us"), who is responsible for your information under this policy. You can reach us any time at getshadowapp@gmail.com.

2. What we collect

Information you give us

  • Your setup answers: the name you'd like Shadow to call you, the personality you choose for Shadow, what you struggle with, your career and main focus, how long you can focus, the exercises you pick and what you can do now, and your training days.
  • Your activity in the app: the goals you complete and when, your focus sessions, your daily results against Shadow, your streak and points, the dares and races you complete, and Shadow's diary entries (which the app writes from your day).

Your account (Sign in with Apple)

  • A unique account identifier from Apple, your email address (or a private relay address if you choose "Hide My Email"), and your name if you choose to share it.

Purchases

  • Whether you have an active subscription or free trial, which plan, and when it renews or ends. Payments are handled entirely by Apple. We never see or store your card or payment details.

Technical information

  • Basic information our service providers need to run the app and process purchases, such as app version, device type, operating system, country or region, and IP address in server logs.

What we don't collect

Shadow does not collect your location, contacts, photos, health data, or advertising identifier, and does not use third-party analytics or advertising tools. Reminders are scheduled on your phone; we don't receive them.

3. How we use it

  • To run Shadow: your goals, Shadow's day, the daily race, focus sessions, your diary, and reminders.
  • To back up your progress and restore it when you sign in on a new phone.
  • To provide your subscription, free trial, and restore purchases.
  • To answer you when you contact support.
  • To keep the app secure and fix problems.

We don't use your information for advertising, and we don't make decisions about you that have legal or similarly significant effects.

Legal bases (for users in the EU and UK): we process your information to provide the service you asked for (performance of a contract), to keep the app secure and working (our legitimate interests), and to meet legal obligations. Where we rely on consent (for example, notifications), you can withdraw it at any time in your phone's settings.

4. Who we share it with

We don't sell or rent your information. We share it only with the service providers that run parts of Shadow for us, and only what they need:

We may also share information if required by law, to protect the rights and safety of our users or others, or as part of a sale or transfer of the app (in which case this policy will continue to apply).

5. Where it's stored and how it's protected

Your progress is saved on your phone and backed up with our providers, whose servers may be located outside Canada, including in the United States. Their laws may differ from yours; we use providers that protect data with encryption in transit and strict access controls. Your account data can only be read by your own signed-in account. No method of storage is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information and will tell you, as the law requires, if a breach affects you.

6. How long we keep it

  • On your phone: until you delete the app or use "Start over".
  • In your account: until you delete your account. Deleting it removes your account, progress and diary from our database straight away.
  • Purchase records: Apple and RevenueCat keep transaction records as required for accounting, tax and fraud prevention.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account: in the app, go to Settings → Account → Delete account. You can also email us.
  • Notifications: turn them off anytime in your iPhone's Settings → Notifications → Shadow.
  • Access, correct or get a copy of your information, or ask us to stop using it: email us and we'll respond within 30 days.

Depending on where you live, you may have more rights. In Canada, under PIPEDA, you can access and correct your personal information and complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada. In the EU and UK, under the GDPR, you can also ask for your data to be erased, restricted or moved to another service, object to our processing, and complain to your local data protection authority. In some US states, you have similar rights to know about, access and delete your information. We don't "sell" or "share" personal information as those terms are defined by US state laws.

8. Children

Shadow is not intended for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has given us information, email us and we'll delete it.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If the changes are significant, we'll let you know in the app before they take effect.

10. Contact

Questions, requests or concerns: getshadowapp@gmail.com
