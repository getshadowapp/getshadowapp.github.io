Support

Need a hand? Most answers are below. If not, email us and a real person will reply, usually within 2 days.

How does Shadow work?

Every day you race Shadow across three sections: Spotless (your space), Unrot (focus) and Shadowbox (training). Shadow does its tasks by the real clock, and it isn't perfect, so you can always catch it. Win 2 of the 3 sections to win the day. The result is decided at midnight.

How do I cancel my subscription?
  1. Open your iPhone's Settings.
  2. Tap your name, then Subscriptions.
  3. Tap Shadow, then Cancel Subscription.

Cancel at least 24 hours before your free trial or current period ends to avoid being charged. You keep access until the end of the period you've paid for.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple, so refunds are too. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in, find Shadow and choose "Request a refund".

I already paid. How do I restore my subscription?

On the paywall, tap Restore at the bottom. Make sure you're signed in to the App Store with the same Apple ID you used to subscribe.

I got a new phone. How do I get my progress back?

Install Shadow, tap Log in and sign in with Apple. Your streak, Shadow's diary and your wins come back automatically, as long as you signed in on your old phone.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Settings → Account → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account and everything saved with it. Deleting your account doesn't cancel an App Store subscription, so cancel that separately in Settings → your name → Subscriptions.

I'm not getting reminders from Shadow.

Check iPhone Settings → Notifications → Shadow and make sure notifications are allowed. Shadow doesn't send reminders for things you've already done that day.

Is Shadow medical or fitness advice?

No. Shadow is a motivation app. Talk to a doctor before starting a new exercise routine, and always train within your limits.
